About HealthGraph

Healthcare Knowledge Graph Assistant

HealthGraph is an intelligent assistant that connects natural language queries directly to a Neo4j Graph Database to help you instantly discover certified doctors and healthcare facilities.

Find Verified Specialists

Search naturally for practitioners across 15 clinical fields (e.g. "Cardiologists in South District", "general physicians").

Medical Facility Rosters

Look up doctors working at specific hospitals (e.g. "Apex Advanced Medical Center") or browse clinics by district.

Multi-Hop Knowledge Graph Traversal

If a specialist is not present in a local facility, the AI traverses graph relationships to recommend practitioners in nearby connected centers.
